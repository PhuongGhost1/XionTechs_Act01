> **Purpose:** Landing-page project showcase draft for XionTechs.
>

## 1. Project positioning

### Internal showcase title
**CRM & Customer Operations Platform**

This title is intentionally generic. The landing page should communicate the type of product XionTechs can build without depending on a project/client name.

### One-line description
A business platform that centralizes customer information, operational workflows, internal actions, and connected business data in one place.

### Landing-page message
Show that XionTechs does more than AI demos: the company also builds the core software platforms that businesses use every day.

This project should act as the bridge between:

> **AI + software engineering**

rather than making the entire website look AI-only.

---

## 2. Business context

CRM and customer operations systems typically combine:

- Customer information
- Business entities / records
- Workflow status
- Internal users and permissions
- Search and filtering
- Tasks or follow-up actions
- Integrations with other systems
- Operational reporting

The exact domain of the project should remain generic on the public site unless the company approves a specific industry/client description.

---

## 3. Problems the platform addresses

### Common operational problem
Customer information and internal processes can become fragmented across multiple systems or manual steps.

Typical friction points include:

- Searching for the right customer information
- Tracking customer status
- Maintaining consistent records
- Following operational workflows
- Coordinating between teams
- Connecting CRM data with other enterprise systems

### Platform response
The solution provides a centralized web experience where users can:

- Find records quickly
- View a complete customer context
- Update information
- Move work through workflow states
- Trigger or complete operational actions
- Access connected data from integrated systems

## 5. Major functional areas

### 5.1 Dashboard

Use the dashboard to communicate:

- Current workload
- Customer / operational summaries
- Recent activity
- Action items
- Status distribution

The dashboard screenshot should be visually clean and easy to understand within 2–3 seconds.

### 5.2 Search and listing

A strong CRM showcase should show efficient record discovery.

Possible UI elements:

- Search
- Filters
- Sorting
- Pagination

## 12. Agent implementation guidance

The AI coding/design agent should:

- Treat this as the **business software / engineering** showcase
- Make the UI screenshot the dominant visual
- Use a professional B2B layout
- Keep copy concise
- Tie the visual story to workflow and usability
- Keep technology badges secondary
- Make mobile presentation readable

The agent must not invent:

- Client name
- Customer count
- Revenue or ROI
- Number of users
- Performance metrics
- Industry-specific outcomes
- Unsupported integrations

Use generic wording until actual project evidence is supplied.
